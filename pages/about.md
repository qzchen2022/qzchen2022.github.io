Before I'm a computational scientist, I'm someone shaped by moving across very different places and cultures.

I was born in Hunan Province, China — a region in south-central China known for its fiery cuisine. I earned my bachelor's degree in Beijing, then moved to the United States for a master's in Ohio, and I'm now finishing my PhD in Texas. Living and studying across such varied environments has given me wide-ranging curiosity, both in and outside the lab.
